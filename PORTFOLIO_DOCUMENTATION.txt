=======================================================
PORTFOLIO TRANSFORMATION OVERVIEW - ABHISHEK TIWARI
=======================================================
This document outlines the features, architectural choices, and stylistic upgrades applied to shift the portfolio from a standard student resume to a "Top 1% AI + IoT Research-Focused Personal Brand".

--- 1. DESIGN & THEME ---
• Cinematic Dark Theme: Shifted the core background palette to a true, deep black (#050505 to #101014) combined with soft, volumetric ambient lighting overlays (violet, cyan, and rose gradients). 
• Typography: Driven by 'Inter' and 'Outfit' fonts. Applied a negative letter-spacing (-0.025em) to all H1-H4 headers for a sharper, premium hierarchy.
• Custom UI Effects: Glassmorphism borders, absolute positioned glows, and a universal `slideUpFade` staggered entrance animation spanning the timeline.
• Profile Image Integration: Profile picture sits cleanly in a rounded viewport with a subtle hover zoom, perfectly aligned with the cinematic theme.

--- 2. HERO SECTION ---
• Identity Rewrite: Upgraded the tagline to "Engineering systems at the intersection of AI, behaviour, and real-world data."

  - SIH Co-Leadership.
  - SB Jain Tech Leadership (Hitting Top 12 among 50+ teams).
